How it works

Three moving parts, all working together in the car.

01

Camera + YOLO

Your back camera feeds a YOLOv8 ONNX model running on-device via onnxruntime-web. Four hazard classes (D00, D10, D20, D40) at a few frames per second on any modern phone.

02

Tracker + dedup

An IoU tracker in the browser collapses the same hazard across a few frames into one submission. A short-TTL cache on the server dedups the resurface so you never pay the LLM twice for the same pothole.

03

Agent verdict

Perception then Research then Decision agents cite the exact maintenance procedure from the local corpus and recommend one of four actions: urgent, standard, needs more evidence, or human review.
