RoadGuard
Detection in your browser, triage on the server

Four road damage classes, one triage agent.

RoadGuard classifies the four RDD2022 pavement damage types - D00 longitudinal cracks, D10 transverse cracks, D20 alligator cracking, and D40 potholes - from the back camera or an uploaded image. A tool-using agent then recommends one of four actions: urgent, standard queue, needs more evidence, or human review - grounded in the local maintenance-standard corpus.

How it works

Three moving parts, all working together.

01

Camera or upload + YOLO

Back-camera stream or a still image you upload feeds a YOLOv8 ONNX model running on-device via onnxruntime-web. Four RDD2022 classes - D00, D10, D20, D40 - and nothing else leaves the device.

02

Tracker + dedup

An IoU tracker in the browser collapses the same hazard across a few frames into one submission. A short-TTL cache on the server dedups the resurface so you never pay the LLM twice for the same pothole.

03

Agent verdict

Perception then Research then Decision agents cite the exact maintenance procedure from the local corpus and recommend one of four actions: urgent, standard, needs more evidence, or human review.
